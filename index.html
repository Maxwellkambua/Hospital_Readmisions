Metric Definitions

30-Day Readmission Rate — Share of index discharges followed by an unplanned acute readmission within 30 days, all-cause, any facility in the network.

Expected Readmissions — Sum of predicted probabilities under a national reference model given age, comorbidity burden, prior utilisation, payer, disposition, social risk, and transport access.

Excess Readmission Ratio (ERR) — Actual ÷ Expected. Above 1.0 indicates excess readmissions after risk adjustment.

Charlson Comorbidity Index — Weighted score predicting mortality; correlates strongly with readmission.

Social Risk Score — 0–10 composite of housing stability, food security, transport access, and social support.

Community Health Promoter (CHP) — Kenya's community health cadre providing household-level follow-up. CHP assignment substantially reduces readmission in this model.

SHA / NHIF — Kenya transitioned from NHIF to the Social Health Authority (SHA) in October 2024. Both payer categories are modelled during the transition.

DAMA — Discharged Against Medical Advice. Strong signal for readmission.

Study Phase — Baseline reflects the pre-intervention period; Implementation reflects the period during the quality improvement programme. Sourced from Table 3 of the reference study.

Data Source & Citation

Primary source. Admission and readmission counts in this dashboard are calibrated to Table 3 of:

Ansermino JM, et al. Enrolment, time to IVA, mortality, admissions and readmissions at sites in Kenya. PLOS Digital Health, 2025. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pdig.0000466

Raw numbers used for calibration:
  • Baseline · Intervention site: 255 admitted · 20 readmitted (7.84%)
  • Baseline · Control site: 119 admitted · 13 readmitted (10.92%)
  • Implementation · Intervention: 164 admitted · 21 readmitted (12.80%)
  • Implementation · Control: 148 admitted · 27 readmitted (18.24%)
  • Total: 686 admissions · 81 readmissions · 11.81%
Scaling: counts are multiplied by 10× so the charts have enough granularity for interactive filtering. Readmission rates remain exact — a filter to any phase/site combination reproduces the study percentages precisely.

Patient-level attributes (age, sex, comorbidity burden, LOS, disposition, payer mix, social risk score, individual readmission probability) are synthesized to match Kenyan inpatient distributions. Readmission assignment is weighted by computed risk, so the top-risk table, risk distributions, and heatmaps remain clinically meaningful.

Facilities. Six real Kenyan referral hospitals are modelled (KNH, MTRH, Coast General, Thika, Embu, Kisii). Three are classified as intervention sites and three as control sites to match the study design.

To connect real data, replace loadCohort() in js/data.js with a fetch against your KHIS/DHIS2 API or KenyaEMR. The expected row schema is documented in the README.
